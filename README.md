# 塗潭社區產業、環境與歷史｜口述影像

Tutan community oral histories: industry, environment and history

A series of four Mandarin-language oral-history films about Tutan Village
(塗潭里) in Xindian, New Taipei City: its industries, a debris-flow disaster, the
coke kiln, and the Kukutsu (磺窟) prisoner-of-war camp. They were produced for
新北市陳昌梯醫師山林保育協會 (the New Taipei Chen Chang-Ti Forest Conservation
Association). The interviews were recorded on 5 September 2026. They preserve
local residents' memories, and dates and details follow their personal recollections.

The films have burned-in Traditional Chinese subtitles and can be watched in any
order. 產業篇 covers indigo, camphor, coal mining and citrus farming; 土石流篇 the
landslide and debris flow of 1969 (ROC year 58); 焦炭窯篇 carrying coal and the coke
kiln; and 戰俘營篇 local memories of the camp and the former prisoners who returned.
The Chinese titles follow the client's wording.
